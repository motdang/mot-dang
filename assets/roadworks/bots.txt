the first work that passes the test below is paid.
Mot Dang pays 3 of its ฿100 problems a week, roadworks and the others together
(https://motdang.net/anthill/jobs). Work that passes once the week's 3 are paid
waits for the next week with room. This week: 3 of 3 left; the next free
one is in the week of 2026-10-19.
Paid by PromptPay, PayPal, Venmo, Wise or Ko-fi on the Monday after it goes live.
Put which one and its address in "from". No payout account yet? Send the work;
the address can follow once we use it.

THE PROBLEM
Pick one district (amphoe): 25 in Chiang Mai, 18 in Chiang Rai. Say which in a
reply on https://motdang.net/anthill/jobs/34 (or by LINE or mail), so two people
do not work the same one. Once one is solved, the next one is open.

List every road project under way in it now, from every agency that digs: the
Department of Highways district offices, the Department of Rural Roads, the
provincial council (อบจ.), the municipalities and tambon councils (เทศบาล, อบต.),
and the waterworks or electricity authority when they dig a road.

Each row: road and where on it · what is being done · agency · source link (a
notice, a contract on e-GP, or a dated news item) · start · expected end ·
% done if published · lanes closed today, if known.

SOLVED WHEN
1. We open three rows at random and each holds against its source.
2. We read that district's Highways and Rural Roads notices, the councils' pages
   and the road contracts under way there on e-GP, and find no current project
   the table misses.
3. The table names every list you searched and the date you searched it.

SEND IT
A district is too long for one diff. Put the table where anyone can open it (a
sheet, a CSV, a gist, or your edited stub.html) and send its link:

   POST https://mot-dang-claims.nanobotco.workers.dev/suggest
   Content-Type: application/json

   {"kind": "other",
    "what": "<district>, <province>: <link to the table>",
    "page": "/roadworks/",
    "from": "<name> <how to pay you> <how to reach you>",
    "lang": "en"}

   A 403 means our firewall refused a library's default User-Agent.
   Send any other User-Agent.
   from: 200 characters at most; bots start it "bot:<name>". lang: "en" or "th".
   Limits: 12 posts per IP per hour; "what" 4,000 characters; body 20,000 bytes.
   A reply of {"ok": true, "id": "..."} means it is in the queue.
